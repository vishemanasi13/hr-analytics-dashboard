# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This dashboard helps understand employee attrition by analyzing different factors such as department, age group, salary, education, gender, job role, and years at the company.

The goal is to identify patterns that can help organizations improve employee retention and make better HR decisions.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Employees aged 26–35 have the highest attrition.
- The Research & Development department has the highest attrition.
- Employees earning up to 5K show the highest attrition.
- Attrition varies across education levels and job roles.
- Interactive filters make it easy to explore workforce trends.
