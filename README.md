# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce trends using Microsoft Excel and Power BI. The dashboard helps identify attrition patterns across departments, age groups, salary slabs, education, and job roles.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

- High Risk Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Salary
- Average Tenure
- Early Attrition Rate
- Department Filter
- Salary Slab Filter
- Gender Filter

---

## 📊 Visualizations

- Attrition by Education
- Attrition by Age Group
- Job Satisfaction by Job Role
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Department-wise Attrition

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Employees aged 26–35 have the highest attrition.
- The Research & Development department has the highest attrition.
- Employees earning up to 5K show the highest attrition.
- Attrition varies across education levels and job roles.
- Interactive filters make it easy to explore workforce trends.
